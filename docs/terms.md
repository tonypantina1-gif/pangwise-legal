## 1. What the app does

Pangwise is a meal-timing planner. It helps you plan when you eat (for example breakfast, lunch and dinner) around your day. If you allow it, it reads your calendar to fit your eating hours around meals, suggest eating before busy stretches of meetings, and show when you have tended to feel hungry. It also offers an optional hunger check-in with a breathing guide.

Pangwise is a planning and organisation tool. It is not a diet, weight-loss, fasting or health programme.
