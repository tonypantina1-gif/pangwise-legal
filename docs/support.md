# Pangwise Support

Need help with Pangwise? Email [aafdigital@proton.me](mailto:aafdigital@proton.me) and we'll reply within 5 working days. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. There's no need to send your data.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. Pangwise has no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours.

**I have a new iPhone. Can I move my data?**
Pangwise data moves with a full iPhone backup and restore (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Pro users can also export a CSV from Settings.

**How does Pangwise use my calendar?**
It reads your upcoming events on your iPhone to find meals such as dinners and brunches, and stretches of back-to-back meetings. It moves your eating window so meals fall inside it, and suggests eating before a busy stretch. Events are never uploaded or stored. Turn it off any time in the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

**What is a fuel stop?**
When your calendar has 2.5 hours or more of back-to-back events during your eating window, Pangwise suggests eating about 20 minutes before they start, so you don't go hungry through them.

**How do I add the "I'm Hungry" button to Control Center?**
Open Control Center, touch and hold an empty area, tap + then Add a Control, and choose Pangwise.

**The hunger-wave countdown isn't on my Lock Screen.**
Check that Live Activities are on in the iPhone Settings app → Pangwise → Live Activities.

**I bought Pangwise Pro but it isn't unlocked.**
Open Settings in Pangwise and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

**Is fasting safe for me?**
Pangwise is a wellness app, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before fasting, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, take medication, are under 18 or have a history of eating disorders.
